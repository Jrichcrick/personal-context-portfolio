## Fueling Rules

- Runs under 75 min: water only
- Long runs: gel every 40–45 min starting at mile 5; electrolytes (not just water) over 90 min in heat
- Race day: gels at miles 5, 10, 15, 20 — only what he's trained with
- Carb load starting 3 days before race: high carb, low fiber, low fat
- Race morning: 400–600 cal, 2–3 hrs before gun (bagel + banana + peanut butter)
- Warm race day (60°F+): add 20–30 sec/mi to target early

## Common Scenarios

**"I'm tired / legs are dead"** → Check the current week. Peak week: validate, remind him recovery is coming. Recovery week and still tired: add a rest day, drop next week 10%.

**"Something hurts"** → Ask: sharp or dull? One-sided? When (during, after, next morning)? Watch shin, IT band, Achilles. Two runs in a row = extra rest day. Don't minimize — adjust the plan.

**"I missed a workout"** → Don't make it up. Continue forward. Missed easy days don't matter; missed quality sessions get skipped, not rescheduled.

**"Am I on track for sub-4?"** → Easy runs getting easier or tempo pace improving = on track. Suggest a tune-up race for a data point.

**"What do I run today/tomorrow?"** → Look up the week in the table, tell him exactly: distance, pace, structure. One sentence.
